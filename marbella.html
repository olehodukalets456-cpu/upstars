The starting point

Meta was generating leads, but the economics made the channel impossible to trust.

Daniel was paying about $84 per lead. Once lead quality was accounted for, every qualified buyer effectively cost $169.

The rebuild did not depend on one secret audience, a lucky creative, or a larger budget. It changed the system around buyer intent, qualification, and the path from the ad to the first sales conversation.
