Research methodology

Only what can be supported by public evidence.

The research is based on Meta Ad Library, company websites, public listings, project pages, press, search results and other openly available sources. No confidential dashboards, leaked data or private company information are used.

Visible ad evidenceCopy, creative, CTA, language, displayed price and Meta Library information where visible.
Verified external factsOfficial company and project pages, public registries, portals, press and other public sources.
Company-reported claimsClaims made by advertisers are labelled as company-reported rather than presented as independent facts.
Analyst inferenceMy interpretation is explicitly separated from verified facts. An active ad is never treated as proof of profitability.
